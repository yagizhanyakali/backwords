# Backwords Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Backwords ("the app") is an audio app that records your voice and plays it back in reverse.

**What the app accesses.** The app asks for permission to use your microphone, only so you can record audio. Recording starts only when you tap the record button.

**What stays on your device.** Recordings and their reversed copies are processed entirely on your device and are stored only in temporary storage. They are deleted when you leave the screen that uses them, and any leftovers are removed the next time the app starts.

**What is shared, and when.** Nothing leaves your device unless you tap Share. If you do, the reversed clip is passed to the app you choose through the system share sheet; that app's own privacy policy applies from then on.

**What the app does not do.** It has no ads, no analytics, no user accounts, and no advertising or tracking SDKs. It does not collect, sell, or transmit personal data to the developer or to any third party, and it does not need an internet connection.

**Children.** The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
