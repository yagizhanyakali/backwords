## English (en-US)

**Short description (max 80):**
Record, reverse and share your voice. No ads, no tracking.

**Full description (max 4000):**
Say it backwards. Sing it backwards. Hear what it really sounds like.

Backwords is a clean, ad-free reverse-audio app. Tap the big button, record up to 30 seconds, and instantly hear your voice played in reverse.

• Live waveform while you record
• Play your clip forwards or backwards with one tap
• Speed control from 0.5x to 2x
• Pitch control to make it deep or squeaky
• Share the reversed clip with friends through any app

CHALLENGE MODE
Can you imitate a backwards sound? Say or sing something, listen to it reversed, practice copying it, then record your imitation. Backwords flips your imitation back so you can hear, side by side, how close you got to the original.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads. No analytics. No account. Your recordings stay on your device and are deleted when you leave the screen, unless you choose to share them.
